ABOUT US

Building the electric fleet India's waterways need

Seagö is an Indian consortium engineering and manufacturing electric marine propulsion — motors and full custom boats — for the rivers, backwaters, and coastlines of India.

Our Story

Seagö designs and manufactures electric outboard motors and custom FRP electric & solar boats — all engineered in-house and built at our facility in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. In just over two years, that range has moved from prototype to fleet: powering India's first electric fishing vessel, tourism boats across Karnataka's heritage waterways, and public transport ferries on busy routes.

Our sales and support base is in Kerala, and our reach now spans 5 states — Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, and Odisha — with fishing cooperatives, tourism operators, and public agencies increasingly looking for a lower-cost, zero-emission alternative to diesel propulsion.

Our Approach

Three things we don't compromise on

Every product we ship reflects the same set of priorities — because a boat that fails on the water isn't a boat you can sell twice.

01

Engineered for Indian Waters

Every motor and hull is built for the depth, load, and duty cycles of Indian rivers, backwaters, and coastlines — not adapted from someone else's design.

02

Manufactured, Not Imported
