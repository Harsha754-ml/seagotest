Case 01 · Maharashtra
Maharashtra · May 2025

Fishing Fleets — India's First Electric Fishing Vessel

In partnership with ICAR-CIFT (Central Institute of Fisheries Technology) Mumbai, Seagö delivered "Vikalpika" — India's first electric fishing vessel — launched in May 2025. The project set out to prove that commercial fishing fleets could move to zero-emission propulsion without giving up the power and duty-cycle fishing crews depend on every day.

Karnataka

Tourism Boats — Hampi, Hubli, Gadag, Bagalkot & Honnavar

Karnataka's heritage waterways around Hampi, Hubli, Gadag, Bagalkot, and Honnavar see heavy tourist traffic — and heavy noise and exhaust from conventional boats. Seagö's electric tourism boats give operators a quieter, exhaust-free ride that better suits sites where visitors come for the scenery and the history, not the sound of an engine.
