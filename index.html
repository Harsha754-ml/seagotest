Proven in the Field

Real deployments, real waterways

From a first-of-its-kind electric fishing vessel to quiet public ferries on busy waterways, Seagö's boats are already at work across India.

Case 01
Maharashtra

Fishing Fleets

Built with ICAR-CIFT Mumbai, "Vikalpika" — India's first electric fishing vessel — proved zero-emission propulsion for commercial fishing crews who depend on real power every day.
